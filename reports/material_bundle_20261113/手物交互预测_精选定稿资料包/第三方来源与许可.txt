THIRD-PARTY NOTICES — Curated Local Research Bundle

This local research bundle contains the selected report, companion, four demonstration videos, derived figures, necessary experiment records, project source and one project LSTM checkpoint for cached-input replay. It is not a mirror of the complete training dataset. The report itself excludes source EgoDemo frames and record-level illustrations.
